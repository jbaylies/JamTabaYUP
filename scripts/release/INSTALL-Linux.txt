JamTabaYUP @VERSION@ for Linux
==============================

A NINJAM client: jam online with musicians around the world. Standalone app
plus VST3 and CLAP plugins. Built on Ubuntu 22.04: runs on Linux with glibc
2.35 or later (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and newer). Use the x86_64
download on a PC and the aarch64 one on 64-bit ARM, such as a Raspberry Pi 4
or 5.

Manual: https://jbaylies.github.io/JamTabaYUP/
Source: https://github.com/jbaylies/JamTabaYUP


Install
-------

The app goes anywhere, such as ~/.local/bin. For the plugins, copy each into
its folder. Create the folder if it doesn't exist.

  JamTabaYUP        anywhere, such as ~/.local/bin
  JamTabaYUP.vst3   ~/.vst3   (Bitwig, Reaper, Ardour)
  JamTabaYUP.clap   ~/.clap   (Bitwig, Reaper)

JamTabaYUP.vst3 is a folder: copy the whole folder. For every user on the
machine, use /usr/lib/vst3 and /usr/lib/clap instead.

From a terminal, in the unpacked folder:

  mkdir -p ~/.vst3 ~/.clap
  cp -r JamTabaYUP.vst3 ~/.vst3/
  cp JamTabaYUP.clap ~/.clap/

Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

The plugins and the app need an X11 or XWayland desktop with OpenGL. The app
plays through ALSA or JACK (PipeWire's JACK and ALSA support work too): choose
your audio interface with the Settings button at the top of the window.


Using the plugin
----------------

Insert it as an effect on the track you play into. JamTabaYUP sends that
track's audio to the jam and plays the other musicians on the same track. Your
DAW already monitors the track, so JamTabaYUP's own monitoring starts off.
