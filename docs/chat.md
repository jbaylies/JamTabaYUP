# Chat and chord progressions

## Chat

Type in the box at the bottom of the chat panel and press Enter. The panel
shows:

- messages, with the sender's name in teal,
- private messages, marked "(private)",
- the server's own messages and the topic, in orange,
- people joining and leaving, tempo changes and connection status, in grey.

Scroll up to read older messages. **Chat** in the top bar hides or shows the
panel.

## Chord progressions

To share the changes of a tune, post them in chat with a bar line between
measures:

```
| Am7 | D7 | Gmaj7 | Cmaj7 |
```

JamTabaYUP shows the measures above the beat bar, spread across the interval,
and lights the measure being played. With 16 beats per interval and four
measures, each measure covers four beats.

A measure can hold several chords, separated by spaces: `| C Am | F G |`.
Chord symbols such as `F#m7b5`, `Bbmaj7/D`, `G7(b9)`, `Dsus4`, `N.C.` and `%`
(repeat) are understood. A line with anything that isn't a chord between the
bars is treated as an ordinary message. The latest progression posted stays
until someone posts another.
