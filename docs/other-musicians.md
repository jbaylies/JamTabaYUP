# Other musicians

Each musician in the room gets a coloured group with their name on top and one
strip per channel they send (some send several, for example guitar and voice).

| control | what it does |
|---|---|
| **RX** | Receive. Turn it off to stop downloading this channel: it saves bandwidth, and the channel goes silent. |
| **M**, **S** | Mute this channel, or solo it (only soloed channels are heard, yours included). |
| fader | This channel's volume in your mix. Double-click for 0 dB. |
| meter | The channel's level as you hear it. |
| pan | Where you hear the channel, left to right. |
| line at the bottom | Flashes green each time a whole interval from this channel has arrived. |

Your mix of each musician (volume, pan, mute) is remembered by name and
channel, so it's back the next time you meet them.

## Why someone is silent at first

A channel starts playing at the start of an interval, with the interval the
musician finished before it. Someone who has just joined, or just turned their
transmit on, is silent for an interval or two.

If a channel's line never flashes, nothing is arriving from it: the musician
isn't transmitting, or their connection can't keep up.
