# Your channel

The strip on the left is you: what you send to the others, and how you hear
yourself.

| control | what it does |
|---|---|
| input button (standalone app) | **In 1+2** sends inputs 1 and 2 as a stereo channel; **In 1** or **In 2** sends one input in mono. In the plugin, the channel is the track's audio. |
| **XMIT** | Transmit. When it's lit (red), what you play is sent to the others. Changes take effect at the next interval, so nobody gets half an interval. |
| **MON** | Monitor: hear your input through JamTabaYUP. Leave it off if your interface or DAW already lets you hear yourself, or you'll hear yourself twice. It starts on in the app and off in the plugin. |
| **M**, **S** | Mute and solo. Mute also silences what you send. |
| fader | Your level, both what you send and what you hear. Double-click for 0 dB; hold Shift for fine steps. |
| meter | Your level after the fader: the bright bar is the average (RMS), the dim bar the peaks, the thin line the recent peak. |
| pan | Where you hear yourself, left to right. It doesn't change what you send. Double-click to centre. |
| line at the bottom | The interval being recorded. Red while transmitting. |

Keep your level healthy but out of the red: the others hear your channel at
the level you send it.

The name under **You** is the channel name you typed in the server browser.
It's sent to the server when you join.
