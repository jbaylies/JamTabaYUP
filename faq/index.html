    FAQ and troubleshooting¶

    Nobody hears me¶

    • Is XMIT lit on your strip? It takes effect at the next interval.
    • Does your meter move when you play? If not, check the input: in the app, Settings at the top of the window and the input button on your strip; in a DAW, the track's input.
    • On macOS, has the app been allowed to use the microphone? See System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.
    • Is your channel muted (M)? Mute silences what you send too.

    I can't hear someone¶

    • Is their RX on and their channel not muted? Is someone else soloed?
    • Does their line flash green each interval? If not, nothing is arriving: they may not be transmitting.
    • They may have only just joined: a channel starts at the next interval.

    I hear myself twice, or with an echo¶

    Turn off MON if your interface or DAW already monitors your input.

    The others say I'm late¶

    Nothing to fix: in NINJAM everyone hears everyone else one interval late, by design. Play in time with the metronome and the beat bar, and it lines up for everyone.

    The public room list is empty¶

    JamTabaYUP fetches it from ninbot.com every 30 seconds. If it can't, the browser says so; check your internet connection, or connect to a server by its address.

    "The server closed the connection" or "The server stopped responding"¶

    The server dropped you: it may be full, restarting, or your connection broke. Join again. Some servers drop you if you don't accept their licence within two minutes.

    The app doesn't open on macOS¶

    See Installing: the builds aren't notarized, so macOS blocks them until you clear the quarantine flag or click Open Anyway.

    Where does the globe get people's locations? Can others see mine?¶

    From the public server list at ninbot.com, which estimates each connected musician's rough location from their internet address and publishes it with the room. JamTabaYUP only shows what the list already publishes; it doesn't look up anyone's address itself. Anyone using a client that reads the list, JamTabaYUP or JamTaba, can see the rough location of people in public rooms, yours included. Private servers that aren't on the list don't appear.

    Can I record the jam?¶

    Record the plugin's output in your DAW. JamTabaYUP doesn't record by itself.
